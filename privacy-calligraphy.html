Privacy Policy for BegucheeCalligraphy

Last Updated: October 7, 2026

BegucheeCalligraphy (the “Application”, “we”, “us”) is a private business application operated by the owner of the BegucheeCalligraphy Etsy shop. It is hosted at etsyagent.afravi.com and is used by its owner to manage the marketing and listings of that shop. This Privacy Policy explains how the Application accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when the owner connects their Etsy shop, Pinterest account, and other services to it.

By connecting an account to BegucheeCalligraphy, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

In short: we access only the data needed to run the features you ask for, we do not sell or share Pinterest data, we do not use it to train machine-learning models, and you can disconnect and request deletion at any time.

1. Information We Access

From Pinterest

When you authorize BegucheeCalligraphy to connect with Pinterest, the Application may access the following information through the Pinterest API, limited to the scopes you grant:

  • Basic information about the connected Pinterest account (for example, the account name and follower count).
  • Your boards and basic board information, so that Pins can be published to the board you choose.
  • Pins you publish through the Application and their performance analytics (such as impressions, saves, and clicks).

Typical scopes requested: user_accounts:read, boards:read, pins:read, and pins:write. The Application does not request more access than the features require.

From Etsy

When the shop owner connects Etsy (via Etsy’s OAuth), the Application may read and update the owner’s own shop listings (titles, tags, descriptions, materials, and public listing statistics such as views and favorites) and may read the owner’s own shop orders to show the owner a customer overview. Customer details that Etsy provides to the shop owner (such as buyer name and order totals) are shown only to the shop owner inside the Application.

From other connected services

If the owner chooses to connect them, the Application may also publish content to, and read account-level statistics (such as follower or member counts) and post performance from, Instagram and Telegram.

Website visitors

The Application is not a public service and does not collect personal information from general visitors. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers. The administrative panel uses a session cookie solely to keep the owner signed in.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed through Pinterest and other connected services is used only to provide the features the user requests, namely:

  • creating and publishing Pins and posts that the owner has reviewed and approved;
  • showing the owner performance statistics for their own content and accounts;
  • preparing a marketing report and suggested content plan for the owner. Plans are suggestions only; nothing is published and no advertising budget is spent without the owner’s explicit approval.

We do not:

  • sell, license, or rent Pinterest data, or make it available to data brokers or advertising networks;
  • use Pinterest content or data to train machine-learning or artificial-intelligence models;
  • use Pinterest data to build profiles of individual people, to track or surveil users, or to make unrelated advertising decisions;
  • use Pinterest data for any purpose other than operating the features described above.

4. Storage, Security, and Retention

  • Access tokens and credentials (including Pinterest and Etsy tokens) are treated as confidential. On the server they are kept in a private configuration file that is not publicly accessible; in the owner’s browser they are kept in the browser’s local storage on the owner’s own device. They are not published or intentionally exposed.
  • Statistics and content (for example, Pin performance figures and the post queue) are stored in the Application’s database on the hosting server and are accessible only to the owner after authentication.
  • Customer overview data from Etsy orders is stored only in the owner’s browser on their own device and is not sent to our server or to third parties.
  • All communication with Pinterest, Etsy, and the Application uses HTTPS.
  • We keep information only as long as needed to provide the requested functionality or to meet legal obligations. Pinterest access tokens are kept until the account is disconnected or the authorization is revoked; performance statistics are kept until the owner deletes them or asks us to.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a security incident affecting data obtained through the Pinterest API, we will act promptly to address it and will notify affected parties and Pinterest as required.

5. Disconnecting Pinterest and Revoking Access

You may disconnect your Pinterest account from BegucheeCalligraphy at any time, and you may also revoke the Application’s authorization in your Pinterest account settings (Settings → Security / Apps you’ve authorized). After an account is disconnected or authorization is revoked, the Application stops requesting new information from Pinterest for that connection and discards the stored Pinterest access token.
